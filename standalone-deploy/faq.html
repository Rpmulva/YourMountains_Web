Frequently Asked Questions

The short answers. If yours isn't here, write to us — a real person replies.

The basics

What is YourMountains?

An outdoor collective. One place to find your next thing to do outside, keep a record of what you have done, and meet the people doing it near you. Ask Claire where to go, track a trail, save a field note, swap gear, and see what is happening this weekend.

Where does it work?

Starting in Summit and Eagle County, growing to Colorado and a mountain town near you. You can use the app anywhere — the local knowledge is deepest where we have started.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the beta. Some features may become paid later, and we will say so clearly before that ever happens.

Who can join?

You must be 18 or older to create an account.

Stamps

What are stamps?

Stamps are what you earn for getting outside. They are not money and you cannot buy them.

How do I earn one?

Track a full trail and share it with the collective. That earns one stamp. If your trail is new to the map, you earn one more.

Why didn't my outing earn a stamp?

Three reasons cover almost all of it.

  • There was no recorded route. A note you write without tracking an outing is still yours to keep, and it still posts — it just does not earn.
  • You saved it privately. Sharing with the collective is the part that earns.
  • It was not a trail activity yet. Right now stamps are for tracked trail outings. Peaks and spots post to the feed like everything else and do not earn yet. That is coming.

Do stamps expire?

No.

I submitted a trail. When will I hear about it?

We review submitted trails by hand, usually within a few days. Your share stamp is yours the moment you share — the review only decides whether the trail joins the map.

Rewards

What can I get?

Right now, the Founders Sticker Pack. The bottle and the hat are coming.

Do I pay for it?

No. You redeem it with stamps you earned. Shipping is free and the total is zero.

How does it work?

Pick your reward, confirm, and we hand you a checkout that is already paid for. Fill in where to send it and we ship it.

Where do you ship?

The United States, for now.

Something went wrong while I was redeeming.

If a redemption cannot finish, we do not take the stamps. If you closed the window before finishing, open the store again and pick up where you left off — your reward is still waiting for you.

My checkout link stopped working.

A reward is held for you for a limited time. If your link expires before you finish, write to us at support@yourmountains.life and we will sort it out.

Privacy and safety

What do you do with my location?

We use it to show you what is near you and to measure a tracked outing. Background location runs only while a recording is active — when you are not recording, we are not following you. The app works fine if you never grant Always Allow. Our Privacy Policy has the full picture.

Someone posted something they should not have.

Report it. Every post, comment, thread, reply and gear listing has a report option, and you can block the person from the same place. Blocking is immediate and it works both ways — their content disappears from your view and yours from theirs. You can manage everyone you have blocked in Settings → Blocked Accounts. Reports go to policy@yourmountains.life and we act on violating content within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account. It is three taps. Your account is deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days.

I did not get a confirmation email.

During the beta we do not send one — you can sign in straight away.

Getting help

How do I reach a person?

Write to support@yourmountains.life, or use Settings → Help in the app to open a support request. We are a small team in Frisco, Colorado, and a real person answers.

I have an idea for the app.

Use Submit Feedback in the app. We read every one of them — this is a beta and what you tell us shapes what we build next.
