Delete your YourMountains account

In the app

Open Settings, tap Delete account, and type DELETE to confirm.

Without the app

Email policy@yourmountains.life with the subject "Data Deletion Request" from the email on your account. We reply within 5 business days and finish within 30 days.

What happens

Your profile locks right away. After 30 days, your Field Notes, Gear Locker, Gear Swap listings, posts and related data are permanently deleted. Sign back in within 30 days to cancel.

What we keep

Your name, handle, email address, sign-up date and deletion date, kept to record the deletion.
